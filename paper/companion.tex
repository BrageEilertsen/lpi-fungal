\begin{abstract}
The companion paper poses fungal-polyketide structure prediction as verifier-grounded inference over
latent biosynthetic programs, and shows the field's accuracy ceiling is a statement about grammar
identifiability rather than data volume. Here we extract the family-agnostic object underneath that
instance: a \textbf{coverage--identifiability calculus}. A sound executor's three-state verdict
partitions any biosynthetic corpus into verified ($\R$), under-observed ($\U$), and out-of-grammar
($\X$) sets, governed by two knobs with opposite monotonicities --- an operator library $\Theta$
(coverage) and an observable set $O$ (resolution). $O$-enrichment moves $\U\to\R$ and can never exit
$\X$; $\Theta$-enrichment moves $\X\to\R\cup\U$ and can demote $\R\to\U$. The asymmetry makes coverage
($\lvert\X\rvert$) and identifiability ($\lvert\U\rvert$) distinct degrees of freedom that trade, and
identifies the honest monotone progress metric as $\Rterm$ (verified under the full operator universe),
not the raw verified count. We prove the verdict is provably terminating --- every cluster resolves
under product, genomic, or trajectory observation, with the within-enzyme product mixture the sole
permanent floor --- and that the observability quantity $\kO$ stratifies into a \textbf{modality ladder}
(product / genomic / trajectory) whose cells are family-invariant. A controllability--modality theorem
shows the design difficulty axes (structural vs control edits) \emph{are} the genome vs trajectory
modalities, deriving rather than positing the field's measured cost structure; and the cross-taxa
prediction wall is re-read as the genome$\to$trajectory modality crossing. We validate the calculus
against the executor on fungal PKS (the partition, both monotonicities, the completeness certificate,
$\Rterm$, the controllability duality) and instantiate its combinatorial half on a non-PKS family
(lanthipeptide, on a provenanced lacticin-481 core), with the structural datapoint held behind an honest
validation-independence gate. Every reported number reproduces from a single documented command.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
The companion paper makes a specific claim about fungal polyketide synthases: the product is the image
$y=\mathrm{Exec}_\Theta(z;G)$ of a latent program $z$, and the field's $51$--$68\%$ accuracy ceiling
reflects grammar identifiability, not sample size. That paper's machinery --- a sound deterministic
executor, a beam verifier returning the exact consistent set $\Zstar$, and a three-state
reconstructibility verdict --- is built for fungal PKS but is, by construction, not specific to them:
only the program generator is family-specific, while the observability and verdict layers are shared.

This paper isolates what is general. We show the three-state verdict, read as a \emph{partition over a
corpus}, obeys a small calculus with two control knobs and a single observability quantity, and that
this calculus (i) makes precise what ``mining a corpus'' means and what its honest progress metric is,
(ii) certifies its own enumeration completeness, (iii) proves a termination property for the verdict,
and (iv) unifies the inference and design halves of the companion paper through a modality refinement
of its identifiability--controllability duality. The fungal instance becomes one occupant of a
family-invariant structure; a second family (lanthipeptide) is instantiated to test cross-family
transfer.

The contribution is a theory layer that travels: any biosynthetic family expressible as a sound
executor over a program grammar inherits the calculus, the termination guarantee, and the modality
ladder. The fungal results of the companion paper validate it; the lanthipeptide results test it on
chemistry the original engine never saw.

\section{The three-state partition and the $(\Theta, O)$ calculus}
\emph{(Full development: \texttt{complete\_miner\_method.md} \S16.1--16.2.)}

For a grammar $G$, operator library $\Theta$, and observable set $O$, write
$\kappa_{\Theta,O}(y)=\lvert Z^\star_\Theta(y;G,O)\rvert$. Each corpus member falls in exactly one of
$\R$ ($\kappa=1$, verified), $\U$ ($\kappa\ge2$, under-observed), $\X$ ($\kappa=0$, out-of-grammar). By
Theorem~1 of the companion paper, $\kappa_{\Theta,O}$ is simultaneously the inference ambiguity and the
design freedom at $y$ --- the partition is read identically by predictor and designer.

A granularity the calculus surfaces immediately: $\kO$ counts \emph{programs} (Theorem~1's equivalence
class), so $\R$ is the program-verified set, distinct from the structure-reachable set. On MIBiG~4.0
fungal PKS this is the reach-9 / verified-8 distinction --- nine structures placed, eight to a unique
program, the ninth (olivetolic) a sound two-route degeneracy.

\paragraph{The two knobs, opposite monotonicities.} $O$-enrichment refines the observable signature, so
$\kappa$ is non-increasing: it moves $\U\to\R$ and leaves $\X$ invariant (you cannot observe your way
out of a coverage gap). $\Theta$-enrichment enlarges the program space, so $\kappa$ is non-decreasing:
it moves $\X\to\R\cup\U$ and can \emph{demote} $\R\to\U$ (a richer operator set can manufacture a second
route to an already-unique structure). Coverage and identifiability are therefore distinct degrees of
freedom that trade; the monotone quantities are $\lvert\X\rvert$ (coverage) and, at fixed $\Theta$,
$\lvert\U\rvert$ (identifiability) --- never $\lvert\R\rvert$.

\paragraph{Coverage flow.} The $\Theta$-move transition matrix has three theorem-forced zeros
($\R\!\not\to\!\X$, $\U\!\not\to\!\R$, $\U\!\not\to\!\X$); the live cells are $\X\to\R$ (clean coverage),
$\X\to\U$ (coverage needing an observable), $\R\to\U$ (ambiguity births). The empirically decisive law:
\textbf{demotion is driven by operator overlap, not growth} --- adding a redundant route demotes, adding
disjoint chemistry grows $\R$ cleanly (\S6).

\section{Reach, $\Rterm$, and a runnable completeness certificate}
\emph{(Full development: \S16.3--16.4.)}

Because $\lvert\R\rvert$ is non-monotone, the honest progress metric is \textbf{$\Rterm$}: cores
verified under the full operator universe $\mathcal U$, not merely the current $\Theta$.
$\lvert\Rterm\rvert$ is monotone non-decreasing in $\Theta$; a core verified only because $\Theta$ is
impoverished (provisional) demotes to $\U$ as $\Theta\to\mathcal U$. The reach-9/verified-8 gap is
exactly this: olivetolic has $\kappa(\cdot;\mathcal U)\ge2$, so it is never terminal ---
$\lvert\Rterm\rvert=8$, stable across the declared beam-adequate universe (terminality is
universe-relative; \S16.4a).

\paragraph{$\kappa$-monotonicity is a completeness certificate.} The beam verifier can only under-count
($\kappa_\beta\le\kappa$), and $\kappa$ is monotone in $\Theta$; so a reported decrease
$\kappa_\beta(\Theta')<\kappa_\beta(\Theta)$ for $\Theta\subseteq\Theta'$ \emph{proves} the larger
search pruned a valid program. The relative-completeness caveat of the companion paper's \S4.1 thereby
becomes a runnable per-core diagnostic --- a zero-false-alarm detector of beam inadequacy, complemented
by exhaustion as the sufficient certificate. This fired in practice (a methylmalonyl-extended scan
inverted $\kappa$ at $\beta=8000$; widening to $5\!\times\!10^4$ restored monotonicity).

\paragraph{Validation independence.} Executor correctness is certified by reconstructing a \emph{known}
structure to a $\simeq$-match --- sound only if the known structure is determined independently of the
executor's inputs. A program-derived (e.g.\ sequence$+$connectivity) anchor is what the executor
consumes, so matching against it is a mirror, not a gate. This is why families with scarce deposited
structures are exactly the U-verdict families: the validation wall and the identifiability wall
coincide.

\section{The modality ladder and the controllability--modality duality}
\emph{(Full development: \S16.7, \S16.13.)}

The observability quantity $\kO$ stratifies into mutually non-refining \textbf{modalities}:
\emph{product} (mass, MS/MS, NMR --- functions of the structure), \emph{genomic}
(domain/sequence/loading-domain --- functions of $\phi(B)$), and \emph{trajectory} (per-cycle
intermediate, isotope provenance). Product-only observation can refine no finer than the fibres of
$\mathrm{Exec}_\Theta$ (which is non-injective); sub-fibre resolution needs a latent modality.

\paragraph{Trajectory-completeness.} A program's provenance-tagged intermediate trajectory determines
it uniquely, so every $\kappa\ge2$ fibre closes under some modality --- there is no permanent
$\U_\infty$; the within-enzyme product mixture (one sequence, one substrate, $\ge2$ products) is the
sole permanent floor, and there the mixture certificate \emph{is} the correct output. Hence the
three-state verdict is provably terminating: every cluster resolves to $\Rterm$, $\U$ (tagged with its
cheapest closing modality), or $\X$.

\paragraph{Controllability--modality (the design half).} A design edit changes a program feature living
in the genome or trajectory modality. The wet-lab capability that realizes it equals the modality of
that feature: structural edits are genome-class (genome-readable, genome-engineerable --- mature
combinatorial biosynthesis), control edits are trajectory-class (genome-invisible, the
iteration-program frontier). This \emph{derives} the companion paper's \S9 design axes and the
\emph{shape} of its super-additive cost (genome edits separable $\to$ linear; trajectory edits coupled
through one reused manifold $\to$ super-additive), rather than positing them. The cross-taxa prediction
wall ($1.00\to0.567$) is the genome$\to$trajectory modality crossing: bacterial modular control closes
at the genome modality (per-module sequence reads the program), fungal iterative control closes only at
trajectory.

\section{Cross-family: family-invariant cells}
\emph{(Full development: \S16.13--16.14.)}

The $(m_{\mathrm{ctrl}},m_{\mathrm{conf}})$ cells are \textbf{family-invariant} --- the design-side face
of the shared observability/verdict layer; what differs across families is which chemistry occupies
which cell. Fungal PKS populate every cell, with cycle-assignment in the
$(\text{trajectory},\text{trajectory})$ cell (the genuine hard, product-neutral $\U$). The framework
predicts a non-PKS family will occupy the same cells with different chemistry.

\paragraph{The cells, measured (\texttt{make design-tag}).} Tagging each edit of the fungal 1-edit
neighbourhood by its program feature's modality, \emph{independently} of \S9's structural/control axis
label, the two are in conflict in \textbf{0 of 192} edits --- Theorem~16.13 confirmed corpus-wide, not
merely on the 6-MSA neighbourhood. Occupancy: $(\text{genome},\text{product})=46$,
$(\text{genome},\text{latent})=3$ (the olivetolic-class product-neutral starter swaps, genome-read),
$(\text{trajectory},\text{product})=69$ (every frontier edit mass-confirmable, matching \S9.3's
``(0,1) verified by accurate mass''), and $(\text{trajectory},\text{trajectory})=\mathbf{0}$. The empty
$(\text{trajectory},\text{trajectory})$ cell is decisive: the iteration-grammar $\U$-class is
\textbf{empirically un-witnessed} in the reachable neighbourhood --- confirming, not asserting, the
coverage-gating \S16.12 pre-registers (it lives beyond the current boundary, in $\X$).

\paragraph{Lanthipeptide (RiPP), pre-registered and partly validated.} Core-residue identity is
genome-modality (read off the ORF). Ring topology is the
$(\text{trajectory-control},\text{product-confirm})$ cell --- genome-prediction-limited and
design-limited (Repka's barrier), yet product-resolvable (the bridges are bonds). Because lanthionine
bridging is mass-neutral, every topology of a core is an isomer: $\kappa_{\mathrm{mass}}=$ (number of
legal pairings), so the topology-$\U$ fibre is \emph{factorial} in ring count --- derived, not asserted.
The combinatorial half is validated on a real provenanced core (lacticin 481); the structural
$\simeq$-datapoint is held behind the validation-independence gate (no independent deposited atomic
structure exists for the 27-mer), which is itself consistent with the U-verdict.

\section{Limitations and the two pre-registered forward edges}
The calculus is established without external inputs; what remains is gated and enters as falsifiable
pre-registration with turnkey executors behind it.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Macrolactonization (the iteration-grammar trajectory-$\U$ witness).} Predicts a small but
nonzero count of same-product / same-starter / different-cycle fibres among newly-reachable HR
macrolactone cores --- the first live occupant of the $(\text{trajectory},\text{trajectory})$ cell.
Gated on a sound macrolactonization operator (the rung-2/3 ``cost-open'' ring chemistry).
\item \textbf{Lanthipeptide structural datapoint.} Predicts ring topology lands in
$(\text{trajectory-control},\text{product-confirm})$, MS/MS/NMR-resolvable, genome-prediction-limited.
Gated on an independent deposited atomic structure clearing the $\simeq$-gate; the scaffold
(enumeration $+$ partition harness) is built and suite-green, the structural renderers stubbed behind
tripwire tests.
\end{itemize}

Four live falsifiers attach to the lanthipeptide prediction; the macrolactonization climb is certified
by the same $\kappa$-monotonicity completeness check. Neither claims a datapoint before its gate clears.

\section{Conclusion}
A sound executor over a biosynthetic program grammar carries more than a predictor: its three-state
verdict is a partition obeying a two-knob calculus in which coverage and identifiability are distinct,
trading quantities; whose honest progress metric is $\Rterm$; whose enumeration self-certifies by
$\kappa$-monotonicity; and whose verdict provably terminates, with the within-enzyme mixture the only
permanent floor. The observability quantity stratifies into a family-invariant modality ladder that
unifies the inference and design halves of the companion paper --- the cross-taxa prediction wall and
the design-difficulty axes are one quantity read in two modalities. The fungal instance validates the
calculus; lanthipeptide tests it on a second family. What the framework cannot yet claim, it
pre-registers, behind a validation-independence gate that ties the limits of what is buildable to the
limits of what is independently observable --- the same wall, named honestly.
