\noindent Dear Editors,

I am pleased to submit my manuscript, ``Sound Biosynthetic Design over Iterative Grammars: A
Realizability Geometry for Fungal Polyketides,'' for consideration as a Research Article in
\textit{Cell Systems}.

Predicting a fungal natural product from its biosynthetic gene cluster has stalled at 51--68\%
balanced accuracy, and pooling a thousand bacterial clusters with the few hundred fungal ones barely
moves that ceiling. This manuscript argues the target is mis-specified. A product is the image of a
latent biosynthetic program executed under a domain-derived grammar, so the ceiling is grammar
\emph{identifiability}---how much of the program the observables pin down---not data volume. Recast
this way, prediction and design become one quantity: the programs consistent with an observation and
the programs an engineer may specify toward a target are the same observability class. The manuscript
proves this as an identifiability--controllability duality, so a single engine serves both directions.

Concretely, the work builds a sound, grammar-agnostic inverse compiler. A deterministic executor
renders a program to a structure; a beam verifier returns the exact consistent program set as a typed
verdict---\emph{verified}, \emph{under-observed}, or \emph{out-of-grammar}---where the under-observed
state names the next observable that would collapse the ambiguity. Run in inference over the 326 fungal
PKS clusters in MIBiG~4.0, exact reconstruction reaches nine cores; a corpus-wide census establishes
this as a genuine boundary of the current sound grammar rather than a low score, and sorts the
remainder onto a located expressibility ladder. In the design direction, the same engine places a
target on a realizability geometry scored by a literature-curated cost that encodes the field's
measured engineering difficulty, and a planner ranks the experiment that most collapses the candidate
set per unit cost. The observability and verdict layers are family-agnostic, demonstrated unchanged
across PKS, a non-ribosomal peptide (NRPS) grammar, and a typed PKS--NRPS hybrid.

The work suits the quantitative, computational readership of \textit{Cell Systems}: its contribution is
a systems-level principle---an observability quantity that unifies prediction and design---instantiated
in an open, reproducible tool and grounded in a sound verifier rather than a single accuracy figure. I
am explicit about scope: the engine is a metabologenomics triage tool that names the clusters where
accurate mass and tandem mass spectrometry are insufficient and structure elucidation is required, not
a replacement for elucidation.

The manuscript is original, has not been published, and is not under consideration elsewhere. All code,
data, and analyses are openly available, and every reported number reproduces from a single documented
command (\url{https://github.com/BrageEilertsen/lpi-fungal}). I am the sole author and declare no
competing interests. I would gladly suggest reviewers on request.
