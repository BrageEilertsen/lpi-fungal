\section{Empirical degeneracy resolution: a dual-engine policy over the candidate set}
\label{sec:structural-rescue}

The Outlook (\S\ref{sec:pomdp}) predicts that the fungal per-cycle policy is
\(a_t \sim P(a_t \mid G, s_t)\): the static enzyme \(G\) and an evolving substrate state
\(s_t\) jointly determine the action. We take a first empirical step toward that
injection, and the result is sharper than a single ``joint necessity'' claim: the same
loss decomposes into \emph{two complementary resolution engines}, each precisely
applicable to a different chemical regime of the candidate set. The fungal evidence here is
preliminary---three detailed assets over an $n{=}62$ conformational sub-corpus---and is the directional
complement to the statistically solid bacterial cross-taxa control of \S\ref{sec:bacterial-cap}, not a
co-equal validation: the substrate-prefix engine \emph{recovers} the authentic program for two of them
(isoterrein, asperlin), while citrinin is a symmetry-breaking demonstration, not a recovery---its
authentic product is out of grammar at this formula.

\paragraph{The corpus.} We extend the curated $30$-cycle HR/PR set (\S\ref{sec:benchmark})
by mining MIBiG fungal PKS clusters whose engine-derived $|\zstar|$ at the
metabologenomics observable set (alphabet $+$ molecular formula) lands in the
constrained mode, $|\zstar|\le 10$. An RDKit-driven SMILES-to-formula recovery pass
rescues the $112$ MIBiG entries without a registered formula; the tractable inventory
fixes at $74$ clusters and the $|\zstar|$ histogram is bi-modal with an empty middle at
$|\zstar|\in[11,24]$ across all $74$---a structural cliff in the natural manifold
(\S\ref{sec:manifold}) that survives the recovery and is not a data-hygiene artifact. These
$|\zstar|$ are at the $20{,}000$-program enumeration cap; the exhaustion-certified, uncapped
per-cluster values for the producible cores---a few of which land inside the nominal
$[11,24]$ gap once the cap is lifted---are reported in \S\ref{sec:pomdp}.
Of the $74$, $7$ land at $|\zstar|=1$ (joining the curated $30$) and $9$ at
$2\le|\zstar|\le 10$ contribute the silver tier. A verifier-constrained marginal-
likelihood loss \(\mathcal{L}(\theta)= -\sum_B \log \sum_{z\in \zstar(B)} \pi_\theta(z\mid B)\)
collapses to standard cross-entropy on the gold and decomposes weight over candidate
trajectories on silver.

\paragraph{Structural conditioning.} For each cluster with a real synthase we predict
the KR$+$ACP didomain (or full-length protein for divergent PR-PKS the canonical
PF$08659$ HMM does not detect---e.g.\ citrinin's CitS, whose KR is silent to the
conventional profile even at relaxed thresholds; we fold the $2593$\,aa CitS in full
and rescue the anchor by motif scan). For every \emph{(cluster, candidate, cycle)} we
execute the cycle prefix deterministically, $3$D-embed the ACP-tethered intermediate,
anchor its phosphopantetheine sentinel at the ACP-Ser:OG oxygen, rotate the growing
chain along the local ACP-Ser$\to$protein-centroid axis, and extract a five-dimensional
pose descriptor: $5$\,\AA{} contact count partitioned into polar (N/O/S) and
hydrophobic (C) subsets, ligand buried fraction, and pocket void volume. These run
alongside the substrate-prefix features (cycle index, fractional position, subclass
indicator, running chain length and oxidation counts, previous cycle's reduction rank).

\paragraph{Two engines, one policy.} Three feature regimes on the same $n=62$
conformational sub-corpus reveal a precise partition (Table~\ref{tab:rescue},
Fig.~\ref{fig:rescue}A). The partition is governed not by cluster identity but by the
\emph{symmetry of the reduction-label sequence across the candidates in $\zstar(B)$}.

\emph{Substrate-Prefix Engine (label-distinguishable candidates).} When the candidates
in $\zstar(B)$ differ in their per-cycle reduction labels---e.g., isoterrein's three
candidates $(\mathrm{dh},\mathrm{dh},\mathrm{keto})$, $(\mathrm{dh},\mathrm{keto},\mathrm{dh})$,
$(\mathrm{keto},\mathrm{dh},\mathrm{dh})$ that differ in the cycle index of each DH event,
or asperlin's $5$ candidates that differ similarly---substrate-prefix features alone
concentrate posterior mass: held-out entropy drops to $0.05$ for isoterrein and
$0.38$ for asperlin (against $\log 3=1.10$ and $\log 5=1.61$ ceilings), each placing its
dominant mass on the biologically authentic pathway. The pose
channel adds geometric noise to an architecture the substrate counter has already
decoded.

\emph{Geometric Resolution Engine (label-symmetric candidates).} When the candidates share
an \emph{identical} reduction-label sequence and differ only in the cycle index of a
mass-budget-neutral event---e.g., citrinin's two candidates both reduce to
$(\mathrm{dh},\mathrm{keto},\mathrm{keto},\mathrm{keto},\mathrm{keto})$ and differ only
in which of cycle $1$ or cycle $2$ carries the single C-MeT firing---the marginal-
likelihood gradient is symmetric under $(\text{cand}_1\leftrightarrow\text{cand}_2)$ and
\emph{cannot} distinguish them from any function of substrate prefix alone. Citrinin's
held-out entropy under the substrate channel locks at $0.69 = \log 2$ to numerical
precision. The pose channel alone barely moves it ($0.64$); the static cavity geometry
without temporal context cannot localize which cycle's geometry it is measuring. The
two channels \emph{jointly} drop the entropy to $0.01$, the model committing
${\sim}99.9\%$ of its mass to one of the two candidates. The structural channel supplies the
only signal that can break this label-symmetry---the substrate channel provably cannot---but
both candidates lie outside the grammar's reach at this formula (the in-vivo citrinin program
requires two C-MeT events; see below), so this is disambiguation \emph{within} $\zstar$, not
recovery of the authentic product.

\paragraph{Directional gating: when the in-vivo program is in $\zstar(B)$.}
The two engines establish that the policy commits within $\zstar(B)$; whether the
committed candidate matches in-vivo biology depends on whether the in-vivo program is
in $\zstar(B)$ at all---a question of executor coverage (\S\ref{sec:limits}), not of
the resolution mechanism. The corpus contains both regimes.

For \emph{isoterrein}, whose biosynthesis proceeds through a linear partially-reducing
polyketide before the post-PKS metalloenzyme TerB contracts the chain to the
cyclopentenone, the substrate channel routes posterior $0.801$ to
$(\mathrm{dh},\mathrm{dh},\mathrm{keto})$~hydrolysis---reductions concentrated at the
early cycles, matching the canonical HR-PKS architecture---and posterior $0.0001$ to
the late-reduction $(\mathrm{keto},\mathrm{dh},\mathrm{dh})$ parsimony-best. For
\emph{asperlin}, a hexaketide-derived lactone, the substrate channel selects
$(\mathrm{dh},\mathrm{keto},\mathrm{keto},\mathrm{keto})$~hydrolysis at posterior
$0.845$ and crucially \emph{rejects} the parsimony-best aromatic-release candidate at
posterior ${\sim}0$: asperlin is a lactone, not an aromatic, and the substrate-prefix
weighting under marginal-likelihood training correctly disfavours the wrong ring
topology. Both are directional positives.

For \emph{citrinin}, where the geometric resolution engine commits with posterior $0.999$
to a specific candidate, the literature establishes a different fact:
heterologous-expression studies of CitS yield a \emph{trimethylated} pentaketide
aldehyde requiring \emph{two} C-MeT events (one triketide, one pentaketide)
\citep{storm2017citrinin}. Neither of the two candidates in $\zstar$ at
$\mathrm{C}_{13}\mathrm{H}_{14}\mathrm{O}_5$ contains two C-MeT events---the grammar
cannot synthesize the in-vivo program at this formula. The entropy collapse
demonstrates that the structural channel carries per-cluster discriminating
information, confirming that the directional assignment remains strictly bounded
by symbolic coverage limits rather than neural capacity---an architectural
constraint formally mapped to the executor grammar roadmap in
\S\ref{sec:limits}. The neural head resolves spatial physics within the universe
the symbolic core supplies; expanding that universe is the work of operator
coverage, not of the resolution mechanism.

\subsection{Cross-taxa validation: bacterial control flow encodes $s_t$ in the sequence text}
\label{sec:bacterial-cap}

The dual-engine partition makes a sharp prediction about bacterial modular polyketide
synthases. Where fungal iterative synthases compress per-cycle control into one reusable
catalytic manifold---so $G$ is constant and $s_t$ must be decoded from the substrate
prefix or the static pocket---bacterial type~I modular PKS encode each catalytic step
into its own physical module with its own primary sequence: the temporal index is built
into the sequence text. The dual-engine framework predicts that the substrate channel
alone should suffice on bacterial systems (there are no label-symmetric walls to break),
and adding a structural channel should be largely redundant.

We test the prediction on the ClusterCAD bacterial type~I corpus: $1039$ KR-bearing
modules across $118$ clusters, with per-module reduction state (active KR $\to$ reduced,
silent KR $\to$ \textsc{keto}) as the prediction target. The substrate channel here is
the standard ``domain cartoon'' (a present KR fires); the role of the per-cycle $G$
channel is played by a frozen ESM-2 \citep{esm2} per-module sequence embedding fed to
a linear head---bacterial modular architecture means per-module sequence varies natively,
so the sequence text \emph{is} the temporal index. Three conditions under leave-cluster-out
evaluation (Table~\ref{tab:bacterial-cap}):

The ESM channel alone passes the substrate channel's ceiling by $+10$ on whole-program
recovery, and it does so without any AlphaFold conditioning. The bacterial control flow
is decodable from the per-module sequence because that sequence \emph{is} the temporal
index that the fungal architecture compresses away. This recovery is of the
\emph{authentic} program: the true per-module reduction pattern lies within the candidate
set, so here resolution \emph{is} recovery---the directional-positive regime of isoterrein
and asperlin above---whereas citrinin is the contrasting case, its authentic product
out-of-grammar, where the same machinery can only disambiguate candidates none of which is
biological.

The cross-taxa contrast is precise. \textbf{Bacterial modular PKS encode per-cycle
control flow in the sequence text} (one module per step, per-module sequence variation
$\to$ ESM does the temporal indexing). \textbf{Fungal iterative PKS encode per-cycle
control flow in the pocket geometry} (one sequence reused across cycles $\to$ the
structural channel does the temporal indexing that the bacterial sequence
channel does natively). Both follow the same $a_t \sim P(a_t \mid G, s_t)$ policy
with the channel partition determined by the architectural locus of the temporal index.
The two taxa are dual: bacteria solve the iteration problem by writing the program into
text layout (modular sequence variation); fungi solve it by writing the hardware into
one sequence and using pocket conformation to route the iteration. The dual-engine
framework empirically corroborates both.

\paragraph{Reading.} The per-cycle policy is empirically a joint function of $G$ and
$s_t$, in the form the Outlook predicted, but the decomposition reveals that each
channel does specific work and the architectural locus of $s_t$ determines which channel
does it. For fungal label-symmetric candidates (citrinin), $s_t$ lives in the pocket
conformation around the static synthase, and the structural channel is the only
way in. For fungal label-distinguishable candidates (isoterrein, asperlin) and for
bacterial modular cycles, $s_t$ lives in a channel the substrate/sequence side can read
directly. The neuro-symbolic architecture compounds across both regimes: the sound
executor bounds $\zstar(B)$ so a learned policy rules within a verified set rather than
over an open structure space, and the appropriate channel routes within that set per
the chemistry of the candidates the verifier returns. The result is a policy whose
neural component would have nothing principled to commit to absent the executor, and
whose symbolic core would not distinguish label-symmetric candidates absent the
structural channel---two complementary halves of one neuro-symbolic loop, with the
empirical partition between them traced precisely to where each taxon places its
per-cycle state variable.
