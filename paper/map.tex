\title{\textbf{A located map of the fungal polyketide problem}\\[2pt]
\large boundary, frontier, and coverage, characterized by a sound executor's reach}

\author{Brage Eilertsen\\
\small \texttt{brageei@uio.no}\\
\small \textit{University of Oslo}}

\date{May 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Predicting a fungal natural product from its biosynthetic gene cluster (BGC) is unsolved, but
``unsolved'' has not been a precise statement. We characterize \emph{where} the problem lies and
\emph{what kind} of hardness each piece is, over the MIBiG-deposited fungal PKS manifold ($326$
entries, biased toward well-studied compounds), by the reach of a sound inverse compiler: a
deterministic executor renders a biosynthetic program to a structure, and a verifier returns the
exact consistent set \zstar{} of programs an observation admits~\citep{eilertsen2026preprint}.
The map has three panels. \textbf{(i)~Boundary}: exact reconstruction reaches $8/326$---the boundary
of the \emph{current sound grammar's reach}, not a biological limit on the manifold. \textbf{(ii)~Frontier}:
within the reachable cores, the conformational decision frontier is thin and localized, and exact by
complete enumeration---zero over the non-methylating aromatic interiors, single-digit within the
highly-reducing subclass. \textbf{(iii)~Coverage census}: the out-of-grammar remainder partitions
into an \emph{in-scope} tailoring gap (CHO chemistry a polyketide grammar could in principle reach),
a \emph{heteroatom scope boundary} (a different grammar's problem), and a small inconclusive bucket;
a re-scan with a lifted size cap establishes that the boundary is genuine rather than a tractability
artifact---$0$ of $91$ conclusively tested large cores were reachable. A structural-proxy set-cover
analysis---an explicit upper bound---shows the in-scope hardness is concentrated in a few coarse
families but dominated by non-aromatic ring/macrolactonization and overwhelmingly entangled: $86\%$ of
in-scope cores touch a family whose sound expressibility is unproven, and the soundly-extendable
part---what a soundness-preserving build with no new regiochemistry recovers---is $\approx$one core. The contribution is the located map itself: it partitions the unsolved into \emph{buildable},
\emph{research-bet}, and \emph{different-grammar}, telling the field where to spend effort and what
kind each piece needs---a more useful object, we argue, than a partial solver.
\end{abstract}

% -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\section{The question: where does a sound executor's reach stop?}
\label{sec:framing}
A product is the image $y=\mathrm{Exec}_{\Theta}(z;G)$ of a latent biosynthetic program $z$ executed
under an operator library $\Theta$ and a domain-derived grammar $G$; genome mining is inference over
$z$ from observables, and the field's ceiling is a statement about grammar \emph{identifiability}
rather than data volume~\citep{eilertsen2026preprint}. That framework is the design paper's subject;
here we turn the same machinery on a different question. The executor is \emph{sound}---it builds only
mass- and chemistry-consistent structures, and the verifier rejects the rest---so its set of reachable
products is a precise, certifiable object. We ask: over the MIBiG-deposited fungal PKS manifold, where
does that reach stop, and what \emph{kind} of hardness is each gap?

This is a negative-space characterization, and one distinction governs every number below. The
executor's reach defines what is \emph{reachable}; it is silent about what is not. Out-of-grammar
chemistry does not appear in the reachable set as a hard case---it is \emph{absent} from it entirely.
So every boundary we report is the boundary of the current sound grammar's reach, not a claim about
where the manifold itself stops, and every fraction is exact-over-reachable and silent-about-unreachable.
We say ``the executor's reach,'' never ``the biological limit,'' because the map does not know the latter.

\paragraph{What ``sound'' and ``verified'' each guarantee.} The two carry deliberately-divided weight,
and the division becomes load-bearing once aromatics enter. \emph{Soundness} is the executor's
conservation guarantee together with the verifier's formula prefilter: every structure built is mass-
and chemistry-consistent, and formula-inconsistent candidates are rejected---exactly as strong as
stated, no weaker. The \textsc{verified} verdict is \emph{strictly stronger}: graph-isomorphism
($\simeq$) to the true deposited product, not mere mass-consistency. For most chemistry the two travel
together, but for \emph{regiochemically-degenerate} classes---aromatics above all, where ring-oxygen
position is formula-invariant---a wrong-regiochemistry product is fully mass-sound yet not $\simeq$ the
truth. Mass-soundness alone therefore does not certify regiochemistry; $\simeq$ does, and $\simeq$'s
correctness for an aromatic core rests on the encoded closure being chemically right---\emph{curated
domain chemistry}, not a mechanical guarantee. ``Reachable''/\textsc{verified} means $\simeq$-correct
throughout, and for aromatic cores that correctness inherits the verified closure regiochemistry.

% -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Panel 1 --- the boundary (certified)}
\label{sec:boundary}
Run inverse across all $326$ deposited fungal PKS, exact reconstruction reaches $8/326$: the typed
verifier returns a non-empty, exactly-matching \zstar{} for eight cores and a \textsc{verified} /
\textsc{under-observed} / \textsc{out-of-grammar} verdict for the rest~\citep{eilertsen2026preprint}.
This $8/326$ is the boundary of the current sound grammar's reach---more precisely, of the sound
grammar \emph{plus the curated regiochemistry} of its encoded closures: orsellinic and 6-MSA are
reachable because their aromatic regiochemistry was author-verified and encoded, so the eight are
$\simeq$-exact, inheriting that curated correctness rather than pure mass-soundness (the honest form
of G1). We do not re-derive it here; we
take it as the definition of ``reachable'' that the other two panels stand on, and we carry its two
caveats forward unchanged: the corpus is deposited and study-biased (G2), and the count is exact over
what the grammar reaches and silent about what it does not (G1).

% -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Panel 2 --- the frontier (certified)}
\label{sec:frontier}
\emph{Within} the reachable cores, how much of the per-cycle decision problem is genuinely
conformational---resolvable only by a pocket coordinate a synthase-local view cannot supply? Walking
the consistent set as a prefix trie and enumerating to exhaustion on the distinct reachable chemistries
shows this frontier is thin and \emph{localized}, and exact rather than cap-limited: it is zero over
the non-methylating aromatic interiors (label-symmetry is structurally impossible without a methylation
firing) and single-digit within the highly-reducing subclass, sharpest in the small,
methylation-position-ambiguous clusters~\citep{eilertsen2026preprint}. We import the result as the
second face of the map and note only its governing caveat (G1): it is exact over \emph{reachable}
cores; a larger conformational frontier can hide behind the coverage boundary that Panel~3 measures,
among the cores the grammar cannot yet build.

% -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Panel 3 --- the coverage census}
\label{sec:census}
The new work of this paper is to characterize the $318$ non-reachable cores: not merely to count them,
but to sort them by \emph{what kind} of operator each is missing, and to size how concentrated that
demand is.

\paragraph{The partition (certified).} Table~\ref{tab:partition} gives it. Of $326$ deposited cores,
$8$ are reachable; $206$ are CHO chemistry the polyketide grammar could in principle reach but does not
(the \emph{in-scope} gap); $112$ contain non-CHO atoms (N, halogen, S) and so need machinery outside
the polyketide reduction grammar entirely (the \emph{scope boundary}). These are different questions
and we never blend them.

\paragraph{The boundary is genuine, not a scan artifact.} The reachability scan caps exact-match
search at $C{\le}20$ for tractability, so $94$ large cores were initially untested. We re-scanned all
$94$ with the cap lifted to $C80$. \emph{Zero} of the $91$ conclusively tested large cores were
reachable (the remaining $3$ exhausted the reduced search budget and are held as inconclusive, not
folded into ``unreachable''). The size cap was concealing no reachable cores: the out-of-grammar counts
are genuine gaps in the grammar's reach, not a tractability limit. This forecloses the most immediate
objection to Panel~1---that the boundary is an artifact of where we stopped scanning---and we state it
as the result it is.

\paragraph{Where the in-scope hardness lives (roadmap; structural-proxy upper bound).} We tag each
in-scope core by the coarse operator class(es) its structure requires---\emph{aromatic\_cyclization},
\emph{oxidative\_tailoring}, \emph{non\_aromatic\_ring/rearrangement}, \emph{prenylation}, and two
catch-alls---anchoring the oxidation tag to the grammar's formula budget (a core that becomes
formula-feasible after removing $k$ oxygens has an oxidation gap, not a topology gap). A greedy
set-cover over these classes (Table~\ref{tab:cover}) is steep: a few coarse families proxy-cover the
bulk. \textbf{This curve is a structural-feature proxy and an explicit upper bound on recovery}: a core
counts as proxy-recovered when all its tagged classes are present, which is necessary but not
sufficient---the tag does not guarantee the operator can be written \emph{soundly}, and the
non-aromatic families are coarse grab-bags that will fracture into several real operators. The
steepness measures how few \emph{chemical families} the demand falls into, not how few operators a
solution needs. The dominant family is \emph{non-aromatic ring / macrolactonization}---present in $176$
of $206$ cores, the resorcylic-acid lactones (zearalenone, hypothemycin) and mycophenolic acid among
them---not aromatic cyclization, and it is a hard, soundness-open class.\footnote{The operator-class
tagger's ring detection was corrected to count heterocyclic (lactone) rings, not only all-carbon
rings; this reclassified the resorcylic-acid-lactone family from clean-aromatic to
aromatic-plus-macrolactonization (entangled), reducing the clean single-class count from $76$ to $23$
and raising the entangled fraction from $19\%$ to $47\%$. The corrected figures are reported
throughout; the correction moved the count in the conservative direction (less buildable, more
entangled).}

\paragraph{How much of the concentrated part is clean (roadmap; the decisive split).} Steepness alone
is misleading, because the proxy is loosest on cores that need more than one class. We fix one
canonical, mutually-exclusive decomposition of the $206$ (Table~\ref{tab:mece}), tagging the
\emph{hard, possibly-unsound} families as \{non-aromatic ring/rearrangement, oxidative tailoring,
formula change\}; aromatic cyclization is the clean buildable class, and other-topology / prenylation
are held as \emph{soft}---meaning only \emph{not the rearrangement/oxidation soundness wall} (a
candidate new release mode and a discrete known addition, respectively), with their own sound
expressibility \emph{otherwise unassessed}, so they do not silently join the buildable count. The $3$ inconclusive cores (Table~\ref{tab:partition})
are included here, tagged by structure despite inconclusive reachability. From this one partition the
headline views follow---and they \emph{overlap}; they are projections, not addends: the
\textbf{clean single-class aromatic} pile is $23$ ($11\%$); cores that \textbf{touch a hard family}
number $178$ ($86\%=83$ single-hard $+\,85$ aromatic-entangled $+\,10$ other-entangled); cores
\textbf{entangled} across two or more classes number $97$ ($47\%=85+12$). So building the aromatic
operators soundly recovers \emph{at most} the $23$ clean cores; the $85$ aromatic-but-entangled need a
hard operator too and may not be soundly expressible. That $86\%$ of in-scope cores touch a hard,
soundness-open family---not the curve's steepness---is what governs whether solving the in-scope core
is a finite grind or stalls, and it says: mostly it stalls.

\paragraph{The scope boundary (heteroatom).} The $112$ non-CHO cores are dominated by nitrogen:
$98$ of the $112$ contain N, of which $90$ need only an N-incorporation operator and $8$ need nitrogen
\emph{plus} a halogen or sulfur operator; halogen appears in $22$ and sulfur in $1$. These are not a failure of the polyketide grammar; they are the target of a
different grammar---NRPS-hybridization and dedicated halogenase/heteroatom machinery---and define the
boundary of pure polyketide space. We report them as that boundary and do not re-annex them into
``fungal PKS.''

\section{The located next build}
\label{sec:roadmap}
The census specifies the roadmap, and it specifies it as three different \emph{kinds} of work.

\paragraph{Bounded build: nearly empty.} The one coherent, soundly-expressible in-scope family is
aromatic cyclization---the class where the mass/formula verifier, plus a regiochemistry-aware harness
that gates on graph-isomorphism and verified provenance (\S\ref{sec:framing}), can certify a new
closure. But the corrected census shows it is almost entirely entangled. Of the $206$ in-scope cores
only $23$ ($11\%$) are clean single-class aromatic, and of those only \emph{one} (olivetolic acid) is a
pure verified-mode product recoverable by extending an already-encoded closure; the other $22$ use PT
cyclization modes the executor has not yet encoded. Those modes are finite and sequence-predictable
(\S\ref{sec:procedure}), so closing them is a per-\emph{mode} curation---a handful of classes, each
confirmed against the primary literature once, not $22$ separate decisions---but each is new encoding,
so the safe-extension \emph{measured} ceiling \emph{today} (no new modes added) is $\approx$$1$ core
($\approx$$0.5\%$). The honest floor
today is $8$ reachable plus $0$ clean-buildable-today, and the soundly-extendable gain is a single
core. We report it as that, not as a build to schedule: the encouraging ``aromatic quarter'' of the
first pass was a tagging artifact, and the corrected map says even the buildable-looking family is
mostly entangled with the hard operators below.

\paragraph{Research bet, and now the dominant pile: rearrangement and oxidation.} $86\%$ of in-scope
cores touch a hard family, chiefly \emph{macrolactonization} (non-aromatic ring, in $176$ of $206$),
with oxidative tailoring layered on; these route through operators whose \emph{sound expressibility is
unproven}. This is most of the in-scope gap, not a remainder. We flag it as an open research question,
not as scheduled engineering;
the learned, gene-conditioned operator route---templates licensed by a BGC's tailoring genes, admitted
only when they pass the verifier's mass/formula gate---is the candidate, but whether it covers these
families soundly is exactly what is unknown. If a substantial fraction is soundly inexpressible,
complete genome-to-product prediction is permanently partial, which is a finding to state.

\paragraph{Unassigned residual: the soft classes.} A further $3$ in-scope cores ($1\%$;
Table~\ref{tab:mece}) carry only a soft class (other-topology or prenylation). These are \emph{not yet
assigned} to buildable-versus-research-bet: prenylation is a discrete, plausibly sound addition that
may join the build, while other-topology is the grab-bag that fractures in Phase~1 and could resolve
either way. The three-way roadmap covers the rest; the residual is named here rather than folded into
the buildable count, pending a per-class soundness assessment.

\paragraph{Different grammar: the heteroatom third.} The scope boundary (\S\ref{sec:census}) is handed
to NRPS-hybrid machinery, not addressed here.

\section{The instrument: a decision procedure for sound expressibility}
\label{sec:procedure}
The map locates where the hardness is; the durable object is a procedure \emph{for} adjudicating each
piece---\emph{proposed} here, and demonstrated on the one class the map has actually resolved. An operator $O$ is \textbf{soundly expressible from $\varphi$ iff the
formula-invariant degree of freedom it must resolve is a computable function of the recoverable
features $\varphi(B)$.} Soundness (mass) is free by construction; $\simeq$-correctness is the bite;
the DOF is exactly where $\simeq$ and mass diverge---ring-oxygen position for aromatics, ring size for
macrolactones. The procedure places an operator on a cost ladder, beneath which sits one prior
question, and it reports a verdict only where the evidence reaches one.

\paragraph{Rung 0 --- the $\varphi$-scope prior.} Does the determinant even live in the unit
$\varphi$ ranges over? $\varphi(B)$ is the cluster. If the deciding enzyme is \emph{trans-acting}---a
separate gene that need not be co-clustered---the determinant is outside cluster-$\varphi$, a scope
failure no per-cluster operator can repair. This is asked first because it is cheap, corpus-answerable
from annotations, and \emph{free of deposition bias}: ``is there an annotated releasing domain in this
cluster'' is a structural fact about the deposit, not a chemistry outcome enriched by what gets
published.

\paragraph{The cost ladder.} Fix $\varphi$ once as \emph{the cluster}---the BGC---since the stated
problem is BGC$\to$product; ``recoverable'' means recoverable from the cluster, and that fixing is
also what makes rung~0 well-posed. With $\varphi_{\text{chain}}$ the upstream-determined linear
precursor and the cluster-encoded releasing enzyme contributing its sequence, fold, and dynamics in
turn:
(1)~$f(\varphi_{\text{chain}})$ --- trivial, the chain fixes it;
(2)~$f(\varphi_{\text{chain}},\,\text{enzyme sequence})$ --- \emph{curatable from sequence} (cheap; a
finite rule set keyed to a sequence class, the PT-clade pattern);
(3)~$f(\varphi_{\text{chain}},\,\text{enzyme fold/dynamics})$ but not the sequence directly ---
\emph{recoverable through the fold}: AF2 maps sequence$\to$fold and, in principle, fold$\to$kinetics
by MD/QM, so even kinetic or dynamic selection is a function of the cluster at \emph{escalating,
possibly ruinous} cost---a costly rung~3, not an impossibility;
(4)~\emph{not a function of any cluster feature} $\Rightarrow$ \emph{soundly inexpressible}: no sound
\emph{and} complete grammar exists for the class. Exactly two conditions reach rung~4: (i)~the outcome
is intrinsically a \emph{distribution}---the enzyme makes a genuine mixture, so there is no single
product and no function maps to a non-point (a within-enzyme collision is its signature); (ii)~the
determinant lies \emph{outside the organism's genome} (a host or environmental input). A trans-acting
enzyme encoded \emph{elsewhere in the genome} is not rung~4 but a rung-0 scope failure, and
kinetic/dynamic selection is a costly rung~3---so the impossibility bar is high and narrow, consistent
with the honest ceiling being ``expressible at escalating cost almost everywhere.''
Discriminators: rung~0 by cis-vs-trans annotation; rung~4(i) by a within-enzyme collision (one
sequence, one substrate, $\geq\!2$ products); rung~2-vs-3 by a structure-vs-sequence experiment with
$\varphi_{\text{chain}}$ regressed out. Each discriminator's false reads are catalogued---deposition
bias, phylogenetic confounding, base-rate inflation, low-pLDDT folds, sequence-proxy circularity,
static-fold blindness---because every one of them manufactures the encouraging answer.

\paragraph{Resolved exemplar: aromatic cyclization (rung~2).} The PT domain is integral to the NR-PKS
(rung~0 passes, cis), and---decisively for rung~2---PT cyclization regiochemistry is a \emph{finite,
sequence-predictable} map: fungal PT domains fall into a handful of phylogenetic classes whose
regioselectivity (C2--C7, C4--C9, C6--C11) has been classified \emph{and prospectively verified} from
sequence~\citep{ptregio}. So the formula-invariant DOF is a function of the enzyme's sequence---a
finite rule set, not a per-core lookup. The executor encodes those modes as closures validated under
$\simeq$, and ``literature confirmation'' is a per-\emph{mode} check of the sequence-keyed rule (a
handful of modes), which is what places orsellinic and 6-MSA among the reachable $8$
(\S\ref{sec:boundary}). Aromatic cyclization is therefore a \emph{resolved} rung-2 instance---the
instrument returning a predictive verdict, not buildability once handed the answer.

\paragraph{Located, not yet resolved: macrolactonization.} Rung~0: for the dominant resorcylic-acid-
lactone family the releasing thioesterase is a \emph{cis} C-terminal NR-PKS domain (studied in vitro
as an excised TE), so the determinant is in-cluster and rung~0 passes; the trans-acting minority
(e.g.\ certain polyene macrolides) is the rung-0-failure subset, flaggable from annotations. Rung-4(i)---the
intrinsic-distribution route---is closed: the characterized zearalenone TE is regiospecific on a fixed
substrate (one substrate, one ring), so the outcome is a point, not a mixture. So macrolactonization sits on rungs~2--3---curatable at a cost not yet
measured---and the structure-vs-sequence experiment is the live discriminator. The instrument has thus
\emph{located} macrolactonization (not rung~0, not rung-4-stochastic) without resolving its cost, and
that pending state is reported as pending, not as a verdict.

\section{Contribution}
\label{sec:contribution}
A negative-space characterization is a contribution because it converts ``fungal PKS biosynthesis is
hard'' into an \emph{actionable partition}: it tells every group which fraction is a bounded
engineering build, which is an open research question, and which belongs to a different enzyme family
---where to spend effort and what kind of effort each piece needs---which a partial solver does not.
Beyond the partition, the durable object is the decision procedure (\S\ref{sec:procedure}): a single
criterion---an operator is soundly expressible iff its formula-invariant DOF is a function of
recoverable $\varphi$---with discriminators that place each operator class on a cost ladder,
demonstrated \emph{resolved} on aromatic cyclization (rung~2, the $8$) and \emph{located} on
macrolactonization (rung~0 passed, the stochastic route to inexpressibility closed, cost still open).
We offer it as a \emph{proposed} procedure---a criterion, one worked verdict (aromatics), and one
application in progress (macrolactonization)---not a general adjudicator: its reach across the
remaining classes is a claim to be earned one class at a time, which this paper does not yet make.
The map is complete as a characterization and explicit about where it stops: the boundary and frontier
are certified (exact, exhaustion-checked, over reachable cores), the partition is certified, the
recovery curve is a stated upper bound, and the soundly-extendable part is measured at $\approx$one
core---the rest gated on confirmed regiochemistry and on operators whose soundness is unproven.
Its value is not a prediction accuracy; it is the located statement of what fungal-PKS prediction
\emph{requires}, drawn by a tool sound enough to make ``reachable'' a certifiable word.
